SAFE NUMBERING UPDATE
The requested original-item order/number alignment is applied once on first startup with this version, with a server-side safety copy saved before the change. Existing sign-offs, notes, accounts and history stay in place. History retains the number used when each earlier action was recorded.
Any additional custom/personal items are kept. Their item numbers are not changed. If other numbers are reserved, the original items use their existing set of numbers in ascending order, preventing duplicate references.
After this initial update, changes you make through Content list > Arrange order & numbers are kept across restarts and later deployments. Re-uploading this same update does not reset your later manual arrangement. Full backups include the numbering-update marker and your saved order. Restoring an older backup made before this update applies the requested alignment once as part of upgrading that backup.

STEP 1 - WHO IS THE TRAINER?
1. Open a learning item and choose Step 1: Shown by someone.
2. In Who is the trainer?, choose an active staff member, manager or administrator by name.
3. If the trainer is not listed, choose Other - type a trainer name, then enter their name.
4. Add optional notes and confirm the step. A trainer choice/name is now required for a new Step 1 sign-off.
5. The saved record shows Trainer separately from Signed off by. The server records the actor and time and uses the selected account's saved name, rather than accepting a typed replacement for that staff member.

This records who demonstrated the skill; it does not require a second person to log in or provide a trainer approval. Step 2 keeps its optional colleague name and Step 3 is unchanged. Steps can still be recorded in any order. Existing historical trainer text is retained, and older backups remain supported.

Staff can see the active staff-name choices needed to select a trainer, but still see only their own learning records. The trainer list contains names and usernames only; no passwords, private account settings or other people's learning records are exposed. Disabled accounts do not appear for new sign-offs. Previously recorded trainer names remain in history even if an account is later archived.

INSTALL ON THE EXISTING ONLINE SITE
1. As administrator, download a NEW full backup of the current site before updating.
2. Extract Verwood-Onboarding-Online-Update-6-Full.zip into a temporary folder.
3. Open the extracted Verwood-Onboarding-Online folder. Upload ALL ITS CONTENTS to the SAME onboarding GitHub project folder that currently contains server.cjs and package.json. Replace matching application files. Include the NEW trainers.cjs and curriculum-order.json as well as learning.cjs, public/app.js and all other project files.
   Correct: repository/server.cjs, repository/trainers.cjs, repository/curriculum-order.json
   Wrong: repository/Verwood-Onboarding-Online/server.cjs
   Do not upload the data folder or your backup JSON. Do not create a nested update folder.
4. Commit, then Render > Manual Deploy > Deploy latest commit.
5. Keep the SAME onboarding persistent disk, DATA_DIR=/var/lib/verwood-onboarding and APP_ORIGIN. Do not initialise storage again. Delivery Desk is not modified.
6. Sign in again and refresh the browser. In Content list, verify the order and numbers against CONTENT-NUMBERS.txt. Check New staff onboarding and the new trainer selector. Check that existing accounts, records and the Storage ID are unchanged.
7. Download a new full backup after checking the site. Trainer choices, typed names, numbering and order are included in full backups.

You do not need to restore the supplied backup just to install this update. Restore is a full replacement, not a content merge. Existing live accounts and records are preserved by deploying the application update over the same persistent disk.

For first online installation, use DEPLOY-TO-RENDER.txt. This ZIP does not itself change your live website. Actual Render persistence across a redeploy must be verified on the service before real use.
